# Code of Conduct

overkube follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, in issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere else the project
is discussed. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not accepted. Disagree with
ideas, not with people.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers privately. Reports are handled in confidence.
Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block anyone who does not follow this code, as the
Contributor Covenant describes.
